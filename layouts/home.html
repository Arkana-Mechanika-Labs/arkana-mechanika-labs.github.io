The game

The Holy Roman Empire, around 1400

Not a fantasy kingdom but medieval Germany as its people believed it to be: the saints answer prayers, alchemy works, and the witches and the robber knights are real.

Characters

A life before adventure

Each hero is born into a family and lives a career, five years at a time: a craftsman, a monk, a soldier, an alchemist. Every year brings skills, and in the end costs strength.

Cities

The cities of the Empire

Inns, guilds, banks, churches and markets: the illustrated cards of every city and every encounter, with every choice of the original.

Combat

Real-time battles, with pause

Give orders, pause, and watch your party fight through castles, mines, caves and forests, with the original's rules for every blow, missile and spell.

Travel

An empire to cross

Roads, rivers, forests and mountains, with what waits along them: pilgrims, raubritter, the wild hunt, and the trail of a conspiracy.

What comes next

After 1.0

  • 1.1: translations. The game, its cards and its messages in other languages, with fonts that fit.
  • 1.1: the Fixed game. The 1992 bugs fixed, each by its own switch, with the original always kept as the reference.
  • 1.1: comfort. An in-game settings screen and optional quality-of-life features.
  • 1.2: mods. A mod format and the Darklands Workshop, to make your own content.
