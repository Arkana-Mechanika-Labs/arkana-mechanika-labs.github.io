Version 1.0 is the faithful game: Darklands 483.07 rebuilt, every rule checked against the original program. After 1.0 the project opens up, and the faithful game stays the reference. There are no dates: each release ships when it is ready.

## Three games to choose from

- **Classic**: the 1992 game, its bugs included. Only bugs that damage your saved games, and plain display glitches, are fixed. It stays exactly as 1.0 plays it, and is checked against the original in every release.
- **Fixed**: the same game with the original's bugs fixed, each one by its own switch. It is meant to become the default.
- **Restored**: Fixed, plus what the original holds but never uses and what its designers meant to do, each item with its sources.

Any switch can also be set on its own, and your saved games keep working in the original game wherever the rules allow.

## 1.1: translatable (out now)

[Version 1.1](/news/1.1.0-released/) makes the game translatable:

- **language packs** for every text of the game, card texts included, in Latin, Greek and Cyrillic letters and in Chinese, Japanese and Korean, with fonts that fit and sharp HD fonts;
- the **Darklands Workshop** (beta), the translators' editor, which shows each text as the game draws it and opens the game at the text being translated;
- the **Interface** switch: Comfort, with walking on over the map and a clearer saved-games list, or Classic, with the 1992 map and saved-games screens.

## 1.2: fixed and modular

A large release that reworks the engine once, in order:

- the **Fixed** game, with as many of the original's bugs fixed as we can;
- an **in-game settings screen**, and problem reports that can be replayed exactly;
- a **modular engine**: screens, rules, game data, battles and the world map separated, so they can be changed one at a time;
- the first items of the **Restored** game.

## 1.3: mods

Once the engine work is done, the mod format is fixed and the tools come:

- a mod format for pictures, sounds, fonts, texts, cards and quests, items and other data, rules, battles, the world map and screens. A draft of the specification will be posted for your review on the Discord;
- a checker that explains every problem, a packager, and mods in the launcher;
- editors for each kind of mod in the **Darklands Workshop**, each showing its change in the running game;
- public notes on the original game's file formats.

## How you can help now

- **Play 1.1 and report** anything that plays differently from the original: **Ctrl+F12** in the game packs a report.
- **Share your ideas** for the Fixed game, comfort features and mods on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/HjzWvmHhqZ).
- **Research**: what the designers meant, what the manual or the hint book describes that the game does not do, the history behind places, saints and alchemy.
- **Translate**: download the [Workshop](https://github.com/Arkana-Mechanika-Studios/darklands-restoration/releases/tag/workshop-v1.1.0-beta.1), and tell us on Discord which language you are working on.
- **Make packs**: picture and sound packs already work today.

Contributions are welcome and stay their authors' own. How it all works is on the [Get involved](/community/) page and in the [contributing guide](https://github.com/Arkana-Mechanika-Studios/darklands-restoration/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md).
