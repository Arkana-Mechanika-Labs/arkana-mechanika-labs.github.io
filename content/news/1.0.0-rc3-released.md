The third release candidate of **Darklands Restoration 1.0** is out. It is a short round of fixes from your reports, one day after rc2: the game itself plays as in rc2, and your saved games and settings carry over. Thank you to everyone who played rc2 and wrote to us.

## Card menus answer the keys wherever the pointer is

The 1992 game takes ↑ and ↓ in a card's menu only while the pointer rests over one of its lines, so the mouse wheel, which rc2 turned into those keys, did nothing anywhere else. Now ↑, ↓ and the wheel work wherever the pointer is: they move the pointer onto the next line, which the menu then lights as it does for the mouse, and the number keys 1–9 choose a line. Nothing else changes: the keys only do what your own hand on the mouse would. Players who prefer the original's way find a switch on the launcher's Advanced tab ("Card menu keys anywhere").

## Slower edge scrolling in battles

Shift with the pointer at an edge of the battle view scrolls the view, as in the original. It moved one cell for every picture the screen showed, sixty a second or more, far faster than on the computers of the game's time. It now moves at most ten cells a second, whatever your screen. Shift with the arrow keys is unchanged.

## A sharper Soft filter

The Soft picture filter, rc2's gentle VGA blur, made small text hard to read. It now blends only colours that are near each other: the dithering of fields, stone and skies still melts together as on a 1992 monitor, while text, outlines and figures keep their edges.

## No more flashes of colour

- When a battle started from a card, the card's picture showed for an instant in the battle's colours.
- The saved games list, opened during a battle, flashed with the battle's colour cycling ([issue #2](https://github.com/Arkana-Mechanika-Studios/darklands-restoration/issues/2)); the original stops the cycling first, and so does the restoration now.
- The party information (F6), opened during a battle, flashed the same way. That one is the original's own glitch; the faithful mode the testers use keeps it.

## Also

- The launcher's "Show launcher at every start" is gone: `DarklandsRP Launcher.exe` is the way to the settings, and Shift at start still opens them. Its tooltips now go where they hide nothing else.
- Without SDL 3 (a Linux system without it, or a zip unpacked without `SDL3.dll`) the program now says plainly what is missing and how to get it.

## Faithfulness

None of these changes alters how the game plays. All 1,001 recorded battles and whole-game sessions were replayed against this build and agree with the original, and the [Bugs & Oddities](/formats/original-bugs-and-oddities/) page is synced with it.

## Help us make 1.0

If nothing serious turns up, 1.0 follows with the same game. Just play, and tell us what you find: in the game, **Ctrl+F12** packs the logs and your last saved game into a zip and opens the GitHub issue form (nothing is sent by itself), or come to the [Discord](https://discord.gg/HjzWvmHhqZ). Reports from Linux and the Steam Deck are especially welcome: those builds have still been tried by the automated builds only.
