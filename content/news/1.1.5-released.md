**Darklands Restoration 1.1.5** is a small fix to 1.1, for everyone who loads a game saved outside a city.

- **Groningen after a game loaded in the wilderness.** A game saved on the world map, loaded again and taken straight to
  Groningen showed the city wrong: a stray `Ö` before its name, no Stadtplatz and no Markt on the main street, the inn's
  name missing, a city lord's title left as `CityLordTitle`, and the wrong toll at the gate. The game now reads
  Groningen's own record on arrival, as the original does. Any other city, and Groningen reached after another city, were
  always right. The mistake was ours, there since 0.9.0.
- **Proven against the original.** A new recorded session (a game saved on the map next to Groningen, loaded, then the
  city, its gate and its main street) differed from the original before the fix and agrees now, and a new check reads
  every card's texts after such a load.

Thank you to the player who reported it on GitHub with the game's problem report: the saved game in it made the cause easy
to find.

Your saved games are fine: a game saved in Groningen while it showed wrong is right once loaded in 1.1.5. Your saved games
and settings carry over: unpack 1.1.5 over your 1.1.x or 1.0 folder. The Darklands Workshop is unchanged (1.1.0-beta.3).

All 1,041 recorded battles and whole-game sessions were replayed against this build and agree with the original.
