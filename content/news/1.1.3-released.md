- **The long descriptions can be translated.** A saint's description on the prayer screen, a formula's in the alchemy
  book and on its page, and a city's in the cards that show it stayed in English whatever a language pack said. They
  are now part of a pack, each with the room the screen gives it, and the pack check says when one runs too long.
- **Other alphabets on the alchemy and camp screens.** Their word-wrapped texts drew a pack's Greek, Cyrillic, Chinese,
  Japanese or Korean letters as question marks. They are now drawn like the rest of a pack's text.
- **Darklands Workshop 1.1.0-beta.2:** the editor lists the descriptions with a preview of each in the game's font and
  the room it has, and its preview keeps the game's pixels whole at any Windows display scaling.

Thank you to the translator of the Chinese version, whose precise report on GitHub found both problems!

Without a language pack the game is exactly as before. Everything else is as in [1.1](/news/1.1.0-released/), with the
fixes of 1.1.1 and 1.1.2 included. Your saved games and settings carry over: unpack 1.1.3 over your 1.1.x or 1.0 folder.

All 1,040 recorded battles and whole-game sessions were replayed against this build and agree with the original.
