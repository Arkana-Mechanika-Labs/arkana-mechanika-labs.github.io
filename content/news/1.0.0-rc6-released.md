The sixth release candidate of **Darklands Restoration 1.0** is out. The game plays as in rc5. A tester playing on Linux found two problems, and both are fixed: the music's lower parts faded in and out (on every system), and the Linux build needed an SDL 3 that the system might not have. We'd like you to play this build before it becomes 1.0. Your saved games and settings carry over.

## What changed

- **Fixed: the FM music's lower parts.** With the Sound Blaster Pro 2 or the Pro AudioSpectrum, and with the FM music that plays when MT-32 is chosen without its ROM files, some voices were much too quiet. The lower melody of the character creation's piece came and went: barely audible, then suddenly loud for a moment. Our emulation of the sound chip took part of each four-operator voice from the wrong channel. Each voice now plays at the level the chip gives it. MT-32 music and the AdLib and plain Sound Blaster FM music are unchanged.
- **Linux: SDL 3 comes with the game.** The Linux package now includes `libSDL3.so.0` (SDL 3.4.20, the same version as on Windows) beside the program, so it runs on systems without SDL 3 of their own, such as Ubuntu 24.04 and 22.04 and Debian 12.

## Faithfulness

The testers' faithful mode plays exactly as the original. All 1,028 recorded battles and whole-game sessions were replayed against this build and agree with the original. Neither fix touches the game's rules: the first is in the sound chip emulation, the second in the Linux package.

## Help us make 1.0

If nothing turns up, 1.0 follows with the same game. Just play, and tell us what you find: a crash, something that looks or sounds wrong, or anything that plays differently from rc5 or from the Darklands you remember. In the game, **Ctrl+F12** packs the logs and your last saved game into a zip and opens the GitHub issue form; nothing is sent by itself. Ideas and impressions are welcome on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/HjzWvmHhqZ). Reports from Linux and the Steam Deck are especially welcome.
