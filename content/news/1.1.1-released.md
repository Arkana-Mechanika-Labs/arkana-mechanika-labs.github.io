**Fixed:** with the HD fonts, on cards whose answers appear only after a key press (the raubritter's audience, for
example), faint outlines of the hidden answers showed over the picture before the key. It was reported by a player on
Linux, and happened on every system. The original fonts were not affected. Thank you for the report!

Everything else is as in [1.1](/news/1.1.0-released/). Your saved games and settings carry over: unpack 1.1.1 over your
1.1.0 or 1.0 folder. The Darklands Workshop is unchanged (1.1.0-beta.1) and works with this release.

All 1,040 recorded battles and whole-game sessions were replayed against this build and agree with the original.
