The fourth part of **A History of Darklands** is out.

After shipping the game broken, MicroProse spent seven months repairing it, from version 4 to version 7. Part 4 covers the version numbers you had to press Pause to read, the GEnie conference where Arnold Hendrick and Mike Craighead answered the players, a clue book that ends with an apology, the memory bug that ruined games deep in the mines, saving in the long battles at last, and the players who passed the updates around on the early Internet.

It also shows what the restoration found by comparing versions 6 and 7 function by function: 93 of the 366 functions that run the game's scenes changed. The footage of version 7 was recorded in the restoration.

[Watch it on YouTube](https://youtu.be/hjdk70J3UCY). The sources, pictures and music are listed under the video. All the parts are on the [History](/history/) page.
