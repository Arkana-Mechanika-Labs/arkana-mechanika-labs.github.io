The second release candidate of **Darklands Restoration 1.0** is out. It brings a new launcher, picture filters for large screens, a move to SDL 3, and a round of comfort and faithfulness fixes. The game itself plays as in rc1, with the remaining differences we found in battles closed. Your saved games and settings carry over.

## A new launcher

The settings now open in a painted window of their own, `DarklandsRP Launcher.exe` (on Linux `./DarklandsRP-Launcher`). It has no title bar: drag it by the painting, resize it by its edges. The settings sit on two tabs, problems are explained in tooltips, and the file and folder dialogs start where they make sense. `DarklandsRP.exe` goes straight to the game.

![The new launcher: the release notes on the left, the General tab of the settings on the right](/images/1.0.0-rc2/launcher.jpg)

The **General** tab holds the game folder, the display, the sound and the packs. The **Advanced** tab holds the Roland MT-32 ROMs, a SoundFont of your own for General MIDI packs, and the start-up choices, among them the new mouse wheel.

![The launcher's Advanced tab: Roland ROMs, the SoundFont and the start-up choices](/images/1.0.0-rc2/launcher-advanced.jpg)

## Picture filters

Darklands draws a 320×200 picture; on today's screens it has to be enlarged a lot. You can now choose how. While the Picture row is chosen, the launcher previews each filter on a picture from your own copy of the game.

## Release notes

### New

- **A new launcher.** A painted window without a title bar (drag it by the painting; the edges resize it), the settings on two tabs (General: game folder, display, sound, packs; Advanced: Roland ROMs, SoundFont, start-up), tooltips that give the full text of a problem, and buttons that light up under the pointer. A click on a row only selects it; Browse… or Enter opens the folder or file dialog, which starts in the row's folder or in the program's own. There is now a separate `DarklandsRP Launcher.exe`: it opens the settings; `DarklandsRP.exe` goes straight to the game (Shift at start or `--setup` still open the launcher).
- **Picture filters.** Sharp (the default), Even, Soft, Soft CRT, HQ, CRT and CRT+, with a preview in the launcher made from your own game, `--filter`, and **Ctrl+F11** in the game. An optional slight curvature for the CRT kinds. The game plays the same whatever the filter.
- **The mouse wheel in menus.** In card menus, pop-up boxes, shops, the pack, the load list and the other lists the wheel moves the highlight, like ↑ and ↓; on the map and in battle it does nothing. `--no-mouse-wheel` or the launcher's Advanced tab turn it off.
- **Character creation.** A right click on the chosen attribute or skill lowers it, the mirror of the left click that raises it; + and − now work on every keyboard layout (AZERTY, QWERTZ, the keypad).
- **No console window.** The game opens only its own window; `--log-window` shows the program's messages in a window of their own. Everything is still written to the log of each run.

### Changed

- **SDL 3.** The game now uses SDL 3.4 instead of SDL 2: sharper windows at 125 % and 150 % display scaling, the system's own file and folder dialogs, and the GPU path the filters need. Windows: `SDL3.dll` is included. **Linux: you need SDL 3** (`libSDL3.so.0`, SDL 3.2 or later), which SteamOS, the Steam runtime, Arch, Fedora, Debian 13 and Ubuntu 25.04 or later have; on Ubuntu 24.04 or Debian 12 install it yourself or run the game through Steam.
- **.NET 10.0.14**, with its security fixes.
- **Faster character sheets.** A member's sheet opens at once, also the first time; the scroll's unrolling keeps the original's pace.
- **Buttons stay pressed long enough to see** (at least a tenth of a second), as they did on the computers of the game's time.
- **Settings the launcher has no row for** are no longer dropped when you press Play.
- **Hand-edited saved games** with impossible values (a 13th month, a place outside the map) are refused with a message instead of crashing the game.

### Fixed

- **Battles.** In two kinds of mine fights the party is placed where the original places it; spellcasters choose their targets as the original does; random spots of the party's formation take the original's test results.
- **After the loot screen** a dead member's row keeps the original's frame line.
- **Keys.** + and − reached the game only on some keyboard layouts; Shift with the top row on AZERTY gave symbols instead of digits.

### Faithfulness

Every change to the game's behaviour was checked against the original game running side by side. All 1,001 recorded battles and whole-game sessions agree with the original, played anew live from the original for this build. About 85% of the decision points in the program have now run in such a comparison (90% counting those proven unreachable). Every difference we know of, and every original bug we keep, is listed with its reason in the project's [faithfulness summary](https://github.com/Arkana-Mechanika-Studios/darklands-restoration/blob/master/docs/faithfulness/SUMMARY.md); the original bugs are also on the [Bugs & Oddities](/formats/original-bugs-and-oddities/) page.

## Help us check it

If nothing serious turns up, 1.0 follows with the same game. The play-through checklist, [RC-CHECKLIST.md](https://github.com/Arkana-Mechanika-Studios/darklands-restoration/blob/master/docs/RC-CHECKLIST.md), lists what to play, what to compare and how to report a difference (**Ctrl+F12** in the game, or "make a problem report" in the launcher). Linux and the Steam Deck have not been tried with this build yet: reports from them are especially welcome, on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/HjzWvmHhqZ) or on GitHub.
