With 1.0 the faithful game is done: Darklands 483.07, every rule checked against the original program. Here is what comes after it. The faithful game does not go anywhere: it stays the reference, checked in every release.

## Three games

You will choose between **Classic** (the 1992 game, its bugs included), **Fixed** (the same content with the original's bugs fixed, each by its own switch) and **Restored** (Fixed, plus what the original holds but never uses and what its designers meant to do). Any switch can also be set on its own.

## 1.1: translatable

*Updated November 22, 2026: the plan was re-cut. Translation came first and became 1.1 on its own, with the Darklands Workshop as the translators' editor; the rest moved one release later.* [1.1 is out](/news/1.1.0-released/).

1.1 makes the game translatable: language packs for every text of the game, card texts included, fonts that fit, and the first version of the Darklands Workshop, an editor for translators that shows each change as the game draws it.

## 1.2: fixed and modular

The next release reworks the engine once instead of piece by piece: the Fixed game with as many of the original's bugs fixed as we can, an in-game settings screen, problem reports that replay exactly, an engine whose screens, rules, data, battles and world map can be changed one at a time, and the first items of the Restored game.

## 1.3: mods

When the engine is ready, the mod format is fixed and the tools follow: mods for pictures, sounds, texts, cards and quests, items, rules, battles, the map and the screens, and editors for each in the Darklands Workshop, showing every change in the running game.

**The mod specification is open for review now.** Read the [draft](https://github.com/Arkana-Mechanika-Studios/darklands-restoration/blob/master/docs/modding/SPEC-DRAFT.md) and tell us on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/HjzWvmHhqZ) what you would want to make and what is missing.

## How you can help

Play 1.0 and report anything that differs from the original (Ctrl+F12 in the game). Research what the designers meant and the history behind the game. Tell us which language you would translate. Make picture and sound packs, which work today. The whole plan is on the [Roadmap](/roadmap/) page.
