With 1.0 the faithful game is done: Darklands 483.07, every rule checked against the original program. Here is what comes after it. The faithful game does not go anywhere: it stays the reference, checked in every release.

## Three games

You will choose between **Classic** (the 1992 game, its bugs included), **Fixed** (the same content with the original's bugs fixed, each by its own switch) and **Restored** (Fixed, plus what the original holds but never uses and what its designers meant to do). Any switch can also be set on its own.

## 1.1: fixed, translatable and modular

The next release is a large one, because it reworks the engine once instead of piece by piece: the Fixed game with as many of the original's bugs fixed as we can, an in-game settings screen, problem reports that replay exactly, the game ready for translation with fonts that fit, an engine whose screens, rules, data, battles and world map can be changed one at a time, and the first items of the Restored game.

## 1.2: mods and the Darklands Workshop

When the engine is ready, the mod format is fixed and the tools follow: mods for pictures, sounds, texts, cards and quests, items, rules, battles, the map and the screens, and the Darklands Workshop, one window with an editor for each, showing every change in the running game.

**The mod specification is open for review now.** Read the [draft](https://github.com/Arkana-Mechanika-Studios/darklands-restoration/blob/master/docs/modding/SPEC-DRAFT.md) and tell us on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/HjzWvmHhqZ) what you would want to make and what is missing.

## How you can help

Play 1.0 and report anything that differs from the original (Ctrl+F12 in the game). Research what the designers meant and the history behind the game. Tell us which language you would translate. Make picture and sound packs, which work today. The whole plan is on the [Roadmap](/roadmap/) page.
