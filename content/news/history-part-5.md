The fifth part of **A History of Darklands** is out.

Somewhere in the game's files is a card that says: "Sorry, this option isn't working yet." Part 5 is about what lies behind it: a Germany meant to change as the party travelled, with wars, sieges and the Hussite revolt, and a whole city uprising of 230 scenes, written but never shown. It follows the roads and sea passages beyond the map, Arnold Hendrick's costed plan of February 1992 for Darklands II in Britain, the cities' unfinished doors, the horses the program checks and never uses, why knights fight on foot, and the web of quests that became one main quest.

The clues come from the program the restoration rebuilt, from the game's own message files and the dates written in them, and from what the people who made it have said.

[Watch it on YouTube](https://youtu.be/DM8PyCmnhZY). The sources, pictures and music are listed under the video. All the parts are on the [History](/history/) page.
