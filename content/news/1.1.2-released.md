**Darklands Restoration 1.1.2** is a small fix to 1.1, for the HD fonts.

- **No more thin bits of the original letters** at the edges of the HD letters, on the saved-games list, the party panel,
  the card texts and elsewhere. They showed when the game's picture was not a whole multiple of its original size in the
  window (most full screens, 1920x1080 for example) and with every picture filter but Sharp, which is why they came and
  went as the window changed size.
- **A lighter text shadow:** with the HD fonts, the shadow under the saved-games list's lines is now a light emboss
  instead of a blurry second copy of the text. The original fonts draw it exactly as in 1992.

Thank you to the player on Discord whose close-up screenshots made these easy to track down!

Everything else is as in [1.1](/news/1.1.0-released/), with [1.1.1](/news/1.1.1-released/)'s fix included. Your saved
games and settings carry over: unpack 1.1.2 over your 1.1.x or 1.0 folder. The Darklands Workshop is unchanged
(1.1.0-beta.1) and works with this release.

All 1,040 recorded battles and whole-game sessions were replayed against this build and agree with the original.
