the game's rules unchanged from 1.0. Your saved games and settings carry over.

You need your own copy of Darklands (for example the GOG or Steam release, the English version 483.07). Nothing from the
game is included.

## Darklands in your language

Language packs can now change the words the game writes: the card texts, the screens, the names of items, places and
saints, and the battle messages. They work in Latin, Greek and Cyrillic letters, and in Chinese, Japanese and Korean.
A pack can bring its own fonts, in the game's pixel style or in sharp TrueType, wider labels where a language needs room,
and its own decorated capitals. You choose a pack on the launcher's Advanced tab.

![The church card in Korean, from the Workshop's sample pack](/images/1.1.0/korean-church.jpg)

No full translation ships with 1.1 yet. They will come from translators, and the tool for them is out today too.

## The Darklands Workshop (beta)

The Workshop is the translators' editor, a separate download beside the game. It shows every text as the game draws it,
checks the pack with the game's own check, and opens the game at the text you are translating. It also imports and exports
texts in common formats and helps you work with an AI translator.

It comes with short samples of the game's opening in French, Spanish, Polish, Russian, Greek, Japanese and Korean: play
one in the game, or start your own pack from it.

![The Workshop's start page with its samples](/images/1.1.0/workshop-samples.jpg)

**Watch the tutorial:** [Translating Darklands with the Workshop](YOUTUBE-LINK) (4½ minutes).

It is a beta. If you would like to translate Darklands into your language, download it, read its guide, and say hello on
the [Discord](https://discord.gg/HjzWvmHhqZ).

## Also in 1.1

- **HD fonts for English:** the launcher's new **Fonts** row draws the game's English in sharp, high-resolution fonts.
- **The Interface switch:** **Comfort** (the default) lets the party walk on over the map with a double tap or Shift and a
  direction, and shows a clearer saved-games list. **Classic** keeps the 1992 map and saved-games screens.
- **The character sheet's skill values** now line up, a drawing glitch of the original reported on GitHub.

## Faithfulness

The testers' exact mode still plays exactly as the original. All 1,040 recorded battles and whole-game sessions were
replayed against this build and agree with the original.

## What's next

1.1 became the localisation release. The rest of what was planned with it moves to **1.2**: the original's bugs fixed
behind switches you choose, an in-game settings screen, and the Fixed and Restored games. Mods follow in **1.3**. The
[roadmap](/roadmap/) has the details.
