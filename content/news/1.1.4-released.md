- **The pointer over HD text.** With the HD fonts, the gauntlet moved over a card's text showed under the letters, and the
  letters next to it went missing. It is now drawn over the text.
- **No more leftover text.** Coming back to a card from a store, faint pieces of the store's lines showed over the
  card's picture. The card now takes back its picture whole.
- **For translators:** a check of every text the game draws found texts that stayed English with a language pack, and
  pack texts that lost their letters. Many more texts are now translated (the character generator, the camp, the battle's
  messages, the saved games' places, the names cards copy into their texts, the map's messages), a pack can give the
  game's pronouns its own words, and no Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Greek or Cyrillic letter is drawn as `?` any more.
  The Workshop 1.1.0-beta.3 lists the new texts.

Thank you to the player on Discord whose screenshots showed the HD problems, and to the translator of the Chinese version
whose reports started the check of every text!

Without a font pack or a language pack the game is exactly as before. Your saved games and settings carry over: unpack
1.1.4 over your 1.1.x or 1.0 folder.

All 1,040 recorded battles and whole-game sessions were replayed against this build and agree with the original.
