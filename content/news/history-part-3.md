The third part of **A History of Darklands**, the video series on how MicroProse made its most ambitious role-playing game, is out.

Why did Darklands reach the shops in 1992 with bugs that crashed it, broke its long battles and kept it from being finished? Part 3 follows the missed release date, a newly public MicroProse answering to its shareholders, and a game sent out over the testers' objection. Then come version 4 and the 386 it needed, what the first players found, the reviews of Scorpia and Johnny Wilson, and the returns.

[Watch it on YouTube](https://youtu.be/nPdWONawz-I). The sources, pictures and music are listed under the video. All the parts are on the [History](/history/) page.
