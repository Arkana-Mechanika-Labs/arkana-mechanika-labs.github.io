**Darklands Restoration 1.0** is out: the whole of MicroProse's 1992 Darklands in a new program, faithful to the original and running on today's Windows and Linux, with no DOSBox needed. Thank you to everyone who played the release candidates and reported what they found. Your saved games and settings carry over.

You need your own copy of Darklands (for example the GOG or Steam release, the English version 483.07). Nothing from the game is included.

## What changed since rc6

- **Fixed: saving inside a battle.** Saving inside a battle now stores each party member's slot number exactly as the original does. Before, a game saved in a battle in a newly started game could carry a wrong slot number in the saved file.

## A new website, and the project opens

With 1.0 the website is redesigned. It has a new home for the **[Darklands FAQ 4.0](/community-faq/)**, the players' FAQ of darklands.net brought up to date and checked against the original program, and for **[The History of Darklands](/history/)**, a video series whose first part is coming soon. The project is now open to everyone who wants to help: the **[Get involved](/community/)** page shows the ways in, and the **[roadmap](/roadmap/)** shows what comes next.

## Faithfulness

The testers' faithful mode plays exactly as the original. All 1,029 recorded battles and whole-game sessions were replayed against this build and agree with the original. The original's bugs are kept, except the few that damage a saved game or lose your items, and a handful of plain drawing glitches. Each one is listed with its reason in the [faithfulness documents](https://github.com/Arkana-Mechanika-Studios/darklands-restoration/tree/main/docs/faithfulness) of the source code.

## Reporting a problem

Tell us if you find a crash, something that looks or sounds wrong, or anything that plays differently from the Darklands you remember. In the game, **Ctrl+F12** packs the logs and your last saved game into a zip and opens the GitHub issue form; nothing is sent by itself. Ideas and impressions are welcome on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/HjzWvmHhqZ).
