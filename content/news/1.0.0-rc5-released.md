The fifth release candidate of **Darklands Restoration 1.0** is out. The game plays as in rc4, with one small display fix in battles. Most of what changed is inside: we went through the code name by name, so that it reads clearly for everyone who will work on it after 1.0. We'd like you to play this build before it becomes 1.0. Your saved games and settings carry over.

## What changed

- **Fixed: the battle party panel's order letter.** When you send a member walking with the arrow keys, the 1992 game leaves his row's letter as it was until something redraws that row; ours showed "W" at once. Each row now keeps the letter it was last drawn with, as in the original. Only the drawing changes; the battle itself was always the same.
- **The code, for 1.0.** Fields, methods, constants and comments across the whole game were renamed by what they hold and do, and stale or misplaced comments were fixed. Nothing in how the game plays changes: every test passes, three separate reviews found no change in logic, and every recorded battle and session agrees with the original.

## Faithfulness

Before this release we checked a fresh round of reported oddities of the 1992 game against the original: items a card gives, cleared tombs, a save made during a card, the cell door's text, the dwarf king's door, prayers, the map's west edge and more. None of them showed a difference between our game and the original; the original's own bugs among them are now listed on the Bugs & Oddities page. All 1,028 recorded battles and whole-game sessions were replayed against this build and agree with the original.

## Help us make 1.0

If nothing turns up, 1.0 follows with the same game. Just play, and tell us what you find: a crash, something that looks wrong, or anything that plays differently from rc4 or from the Darklands you remember. In the game, **Ctrl+F12** packs the logs and your last saved game into a zip and opens the GitHub issue form; nothing is sent by itself. Ideas and impressions are welcome on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/HjzWvmHhqZ).
