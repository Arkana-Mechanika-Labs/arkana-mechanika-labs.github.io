The fourth release candidate of **Darklands Restoration 1.0** is out. It fixes one part of the game: what happens when you split the party over several floors of a castle, a mine or another dungeon. Everything else plays as in rc3, and your saved games and settings carry over.

## From one report to a whole audit

A player reported that a member sent alone up the stairs didn't always come back ([issue #3](https://github.com/Arkana-Mechanika-Studios/darklands-restoration/issues/3)). Thanks to their patient, exact steps we found the cause: the order read the stairs of the floor on screen, not of the floor the member stood on. In the original it only worked in their castle by luck.

That made us look at split parties as a whole. The 1992 game keeps only the floor on screen in memory, yet every moment it moves and fights everybody on every floor as if they all stood there. We went through every place where that matters, checked them against the original's code, and ran 1,656 generated battlefields through the same sequences.

## What is fixed

Everything that can lose a member, strand a figure or ruin the battle:

- **Use Stairs and U** bring members back by their own stairs, whoever is selected and whatever floor is shown. The leader no longer jumps to a wrong floor.
- **Doors, chests and locks** answer the member standing at them, not the one with the same number on another floor.
- **Alarms, traps and secret doors** react only to those on their own floor. An alarm no longer ends a battle nobody was near.
- **Enemies see, target and shoot only on their own floor.** They no longer shoot a lone member through the floors, and arrows between figures on a hidden floor hit again.
- **Walking** uses the walker's own floor's walls.
- **Nobody is turned "Away" by a split**, which could make members leave the party for good:
  - one member leaving by an exit while the others took the stairs;
  - a flight with one member alone downstairs;
  - enemies following down the stairs while the screen shows someone left behind.
- **Nobody ends up in a wall or outside the map:**
  - after an out-of-date stairs record;
  - on waking up on a hidden floor;
  - on coming back up in a few generated mines;
  - in a dungeon battle taken up again after a card.
- **A member who dies alone** leaves his pack on his own floor.

We also fixed three bugs of our own in the same area: a crash, traps that didn't re-arm after a floor change, and the screen landing on an empty floor.

## What stays as the original

These are all bugs of the 1992 game, and each fix is a switch: the faithful mode the testers use keeps the original's behaviour, and is checked against it. The split-party oddities that only change how a battle plays, with nothing lost, are kept as the original for 1.0. They are listed with the decision taken for each on the [Bugs & Oddities](/formats/original-bugs-and-oddities/) page, some of them for optional fixes after 1.0.

## Faithfulness

All 1,017 recorded battles and whole-game sessions, including new ones in the reporter's castle, were replayed against this build and agree with the original.

## Help us make 1.0

If nothing serious turns up, 1.0 follows with the same game. Splitting the party in castles and mines is especially worth trying. In the game, **Ctrl+F12** packs the logs and your last saved game into a zip and opens the GitHub issue form (nothing is sent by itself), or come to the [Discord](https://discord.gg/HjzWvmHhqZ). Reports from Linux and the Steam Deck are especially welcome.
