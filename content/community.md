<div class="dlp-intro">
  <div class="dl-kicker">The project is open</div>
  <p class="dlp-lead">With 1.0 the Darklands Restoration Project opens to everyone who wants to help. There is no sign-up and no paperwork: pick what you like, and say hello on the Discord.</p>
  <p>What you make stays your own. Code is free software under the GPL-3.0; for art, sound or text you choose a licence that lets others share it. The one thing the project never takes is the original game's files, or anything copied from them.</p>
  <div class="dlh-actions"><a class="dl-btn dl-btn-primary" href="https://discord.gg/HjzWvmHhqZ" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Join the Discord ↗</a><a class="dl-btn dl-btn-ghost" href="https://github.com/Arkana-Mechanika-Studios/darklands-restoration" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">The code on GitHub ↗</a></div>
</div>

<div class="dlp-ways">
  <section><span class="dlp-num">i</span><h2>Play and report</h2><p>The most useful help of all. Play 1.0, and tell us about a crash, something that looks or sounds wrong, or anything that plays differently from the Darklands you remember.</p><p class="dlp-first"><strong>First step:</strong> in the game, press <strong>Ctrl+F12</strong>. It packs the logs and your last saved game into a zip and opens the GitHub issue form. Nothing is sent by itself.</p></section>
  <section><span class="dlp-num">ii</span><h2>Test on Linux and the Steam Deck</h2><p>The Linux build is new, and every distribution is a little different. Reports from real machines tell us what works.</p><p class="dlp-first"><strong>First step:</strong> follow the Linux part of the <a href="/download/">download page</a>, and tell us your system and what you saw.</p></section>
  <section><span class="dlp-num">iii</span><h2>Translate</h2><p>Version 1.1 makes every text of the game translatable, the card texts included, with fonts that fit. Translators get the test builds first.</p><p class="dlp-first"><strong>First step:</strong> say on the Discord which language you would like to work on.</p></section>
  <section><span class="dlp-num">iv</span><h2>Research</h2><p>What the designers meant, what the manual or the hint book describes that the game never does, the history behind its places, saints and alchemy, and the story of how it was made.</p><p class="dlp-first"><strong>First step:</strong> read <a href="/formats/original-bugs-and-oddities/">Bugs &amp; Oddities</a> and the <a href="/community-faq/">Darklands FAQ</a>, and bring what you know to the Discord.</p></section>
  <section><span class="dlp-num">v</span><h2>Make packs</h2><p>Picture packs and sound packs already work: new art for any picture or sprite, new music and sounds, in plain PNG, OGG, WAV or MIDI files.</p><p class="dlp-first"><strong>First step:</strong> the guide <code>docs/PACKS.md</code> and the example sound pack come with the game.</p></section>
  <section><span class="dlp-num">vi</span><h2>Write code</h2><p>The engine is C# for .NET 10. Every change to the rules is checked against the original program, so the faithful game stays exact.</p><p class="dlp-first"><strong>First step:</strong> read the <a href="https://github.com/Arkana-Mechanika-Studios/darklands-restoration/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">contributing guide</a>, then pick an issue on GitHub.</p></section>
</div>

<div class="dlp-intro" style="margin-top:3rem">
  <h2 class="dl-title">What is coming</h2>
  <p>Translations, the Fixed game and comfort features in 1.1, then mods and the Darklands Workshop in 1.2. The <a href="/roadmap/">roadmap</a> has the details.</p>
