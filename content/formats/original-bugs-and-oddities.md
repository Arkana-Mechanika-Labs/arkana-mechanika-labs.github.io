Darklands has genuine defects, surprising rules, and texts and choices its players never see. The restoration keeps them all, because they are part of the game; this page collects them.

The **reproduced bugs** and the count of **hidden branches** come from the restoration's own registers, where each entry is checked against the original program and cites its place in it. The **reported leads** (reports from players and documentation) and the **earlier findings** of the earlier development have each been re-checked against the restoration's code and registers, and each shows its verdict: in the restoration's registers, not a bug of 483.07 (with the reason), or still open.
