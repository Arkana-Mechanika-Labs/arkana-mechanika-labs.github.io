<div class="dlp-intro">
  <div class="dl-kicker">A video series</div>
  <p class="dlp-lead">How MicroProse, a company of flight simulators and strategy games, came to make its most ambitious role-playing game: the company and its people, the ideas behind Darklands, its troubled release and what became of it.</p>
  <p>The series is told from the sources: interviews with the people who made the game, their papers, the magazines of the time and the players' own records. Each episode lists the sources it draws on.</p>
