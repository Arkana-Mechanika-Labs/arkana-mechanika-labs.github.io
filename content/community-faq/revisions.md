## 7.0 Revision History

### Changes from v1.0 to v1.1 - November 1995

- Correspondence between Erik Novales and Microprose added, concerning a sequel to Darklands (section 1)
- Small addition to AvL's note on defeating Raubritters (section 3)
- Note from AvL to his former bug report, and report about a new bug encounter (section 2)
- Addition to AvL's work on mines' problem iii) (section 4.3)
- A small note on teachers by AvL, included in the 'Alchemy'-section (section 3.10)
- Info on Arnold Hendrick's work at Microprose by CMB (section 1)
- Notes on fighting tactics by FF (section 3.9)
- Notes on character generation by DA (section 3.1 - new section)

### Changes from v1.1 to v1.2 - August 1996

- Reference to Arnold Hendrick having worked on DRAGONQUEST for SPI removed (Section 1). CMB checked his copy and found out it wasn't Arnold.
- Two oddities (accommodation bug/cheat, friendly alchemist bug/cheat) discovered by Gui Terence Ang added to Section 3.
- A nice cheat at the Innkeeper's discovered by Michael Nemeth, added to Section 5.
- An additional WWW link added to Section 2. It's a review at GamesDomain, link to there provided by Matt L. Wirkkala.
- A report on the disappearance of Darklands on Jojo's list, and the resulting discussion between FF and Jojo included in Section 1.
- A note was included in the 'Dragons'-subsection of Section 4, that there is detailed info on dragons available at the end of Section 5.
- Note on teachers moved from Section 3 (Alchemy) to Section 5 (List of cities, cathedrals, universities).
- Reference to another RPG added that Arnold Hendricks was involved in, by CMB in Section 1.

### Changes from v1.2 to v1.21 - October 1996

- Some minor bugs in the HTML code fixed.
- Code-Sheet of DARK LAND, provided as PDF-File, put online. Resulting of that, links in the HTML-Files were included to download the file. (Section 6 and 1)
- Links to mirrors of the Dresden games FTP-Server included. (Section 2)
- A note by Gary E. Bloom on the CD version and its included (or not included) manuals (Section 1)

### Changes from v1.21 to v2.0 - February 1998

- Biggest change is creation of a permanent home at this site (Conk!World)
- Cosmetic changes to the appearance of the FAQ (mostly coding glitches)
- Minor changes to the text (typos, a few minor additions, nothing substantial)
- Removal of version comments inside the text itself
- Addition (hopefully soon) of a section on Running through Win95
- Addition of extra files related to DARK LANDS (DLED.ZIP among others)
- Addition of a What's New section instead of adding comments in text (coming soon)

### Changes from v2.0 to v3.0 - October 2001

- Updated code to XHTML 1.0 standards
- Introduced CSS1/CSS2 to aid in administration and reduce code
- Introduced new pagination scheme
- Introduced new navigation scheme
- Rewrote item 1.3 "Where do I find Darklands now?"
- Added information for running Darklands under Windows 95 and later
- Added information for running Darklands under Linux
- Added information on accurate sound emulation with modern hardware
- Added information on slowing down the game for playability
- Completely reorganized Sections 3, 4, and 5 for more logical flow
- Removed section: "Additional Files"
- Created separate section for cluebook summary (now Section 6)
- Deleted some redundant information
- Added more integration with Darklands website (www.darklands.net)

### Changes from v3.0 to v4.0 - November 2026

Version 4.0 was edited by Arkana Mechanika for the Darklands Restoration Project. The game's rules were checked against the original program, version 483.07, which the restoration has read routine by routine. The new questions are those the players asked most often on the Darklands mailing list, on Steam and on Discord. Every entry of version 3.0 is kept under its old number. Signed notes keep their authors' own words. Where one was wrong, a wrong figure or word was changed in place, and the correction follows the note. [The Classic FAQ, Checked](/classic-faq/) gives the evidence for the main corrections.

**Throughout**

- Wrong statements corrected in place; dead shops, addresses and links replaced; spelling slips fixed.
- New questions answered inside the entries they belong to; new entries numbered after the old ones.
- Pictures from the game and from period woodcuts added; answers about the end of the game folded away.

**Section 1, Background**

- 1.1: Hendrick's years at Heritage, Dwarfstar and Coleco, his start at MicroProse, his later career, his return to Darklands from 2010 and his death in 2020 added. *iM1A2 Abrams* was still being made at the end of 1996, not released in 1995.
- 1.2: the press release dated to 1991 and compared with the game that shipped in August 1992.
- 1.3: GOG (2011) and Steam (2014) in place of the shops that have closed.
- 1.4: the manual's wording on sequels quoted exactly (it names no country); *Computer Gaming World*'s 1994 explanation and Hendrick's later words added.
- New: 1.5 who made Darklands, 1.6 who owns it now, 1.7 remakes and successors, 1.8 what was cut.

**Section 2, Technical assistance**

- 2.1: the versions 483.04 to 483.07, with dates and patch order; the 483.07 README kept in full, followed by the points where it is wrong.
- 2.2: what is now known of the three reported bugs; the bugs players meet most often added, with a link to the Bugs & Oddities page.
- 2.3 to 2.9: file sources updated; the Windows 95, ME, NT and Linux notes kept as history; sound drivers and the Roland speech trick explained; DOSBox in place of MoSlo and VDMSound.
- New: 2.10 running the game today, 2.11 memory errors, 2.12 saving and loading, 2.13 save-and-reload, 2.14 the copy check, 2.15 keys and difficulty, 2.16 manual or clue book, 2.17 translations, 2.18 command-line options.

**Section 3, General game considerations**

- 3.1: a retiring member takes a fifth of the party's cash and bank notes, not of all its wealth. Character creation costs and limits, the aging table, the rarer careers and the ready-made party added.
- 3.2: a raubritter job raises standing by 40 or more. Shop prices, Charisma, the St. Denis trick, and what to do when broke added.
- 3.3: brewing chance, potion quality, the formula shops' bug, the formula exchange, the witch's formulas, ingredients, the philosopher's stone and the weapon and armour potions added, with a table of all 66 formulas.
- 3.4: St. Giles of Portugal costs a point of current Strength and a point of Virtue, not Strength and Endurance for good; about forty saint texts differ from what the saint does. The prayer chance, Divine Favor, Virtue and where to learn saints added.
- 3.5: chainmail is not light; salvaged gear loses ten points of quality; Zürich added to the armourers. Quality, penetration, glancing blows, weapon speed, shields and missiles explained.
- 3.6 to 3.8: the old woman's lodging, the night watch and sneaking corrected; gate tolls, teachers, fame titles, standing, arrest and the dungeon added; the Edged Weapons training bug explained.
- 3.9 and 3.10: town leaders do pay (seven florins per point of city size); St. Cecilia helps only in the town you are in; the Hanse stays 360 days and the village Schulz 90; no miner ever joins.
- 3.11 to 3.13: horses do not make travel faster; fares and sailing added; each extra attacker adds ten points to everyone's chance to hit.
- 3.14: enemy equipment qualities from the game's enemy table; the Lancers of War cost Strength and Endurance only.
- 3.15 and 3.16: age has no effect in play, so the advice to retire characters over 40 is gone; healing speed, the ending and the world after Baphomet added.
- New: 3.17 the quest log.

**Section 4, Specific quests and encounters**

- 4.1 to 4.4: pilgrims and refugees told apart; the hermit's offers and the friendly alchemist's trade corrected; caravans and their odds added.
- 4.5: beating the jousting knight brings no fame or standing.
- 4.6: hermits removed and Dominican friars added among the extortioners; the standing losses and the tolls made exact.
- 4.7 and 4.8: the crypt item bug explained; raubritter pay, castles and the endless raubritter fight added.
- 4.9 to 4.12: mine quests, the stone box and the riddle doors; witches' huts and the beaten witch's offers; how villages fall to the witches; how dragons appear and what reasoning with them does.
- 4.13: the Wild Hunt can be banished for good by the saint the Holzfrau names; its rules and the outrunning bug added.
- 4.14 to 4.16: the High Sabbat gives +3 Virtue; corrections to the Great Monastery walkthrough; Baphomet's castle opens after either the High Sabbat or the fortress, not only after both.
- New: 4.17 the maidens offered to a dragon, 4.18 the sabbats' dates and places, 4.19 tombs and purifying, 4.20 preparing for Baphomet.

**Section 5, Miscellaneous tidbits**

- 5.1: the rope's real uses, the peat bog's rope bug, the grappling hook and the boar's tusk (no use), and what horses do.
- 5.2: relics restore Divine Favor, have no daily limit and can never be sold; Catherine's Pain is a usable 85q poniard.
- 5.4: the cities of the Empire listed for the first time, from the game's data.
- 5.5: the cathedral and university lists corrected (48 and 13).
- 5.6 to 5.8: the castle lord's vision comes from St. Gabriel, Hildegard or Isidore; new tips; known bugs noted beside the "stupid things".

**Section 6, Hint book**

- Wealthy urban childhood, the Preceptor's sword (55q), St. Peter (Virtue 58), city sizes, the brewing formula, the aging example and two dragon lairs corrected.
